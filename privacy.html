The short version

Your cycle stays on your phone.

Cycle information you enter in the Hedhi app is saved locally on your device. Hedhi does not send that information to a Hedhi server, and the app does not provide a Hedhi account or cloud-sync service.

✓ Stored on your device✓ Not sent to Hedhi servers✓ No Hedhi account required

1. Information you enter in the app

Depending on the features you choose to use, Hedhi may let you record information such as:

  • Period dates, cycle length and period duration.
  • Cycle calendar entries and flow observations.
  • Symptoms, pain, mood, energy and wellbeing check-ins.
  • Sleep, notes, tracking preferences and reminder settings.
  • Profile preferences you choose to enter, such as a preferred name or theme.

This information is saved locally within the app on your device. It is used to provide the app's tracking, calendar and insight features.

2. Local storage — no Hedhi server sync

Hedhi does not transmit the cycle and wellbeing information you enter to Hedhi servers. The app does not create a Hedhi account and does not sync your cycle records to a Hedhi cloud database.

Because the information is stored on your device, it is generally available on that device rather than automatically appearing on another phone. If you change or lose your device, your records may not move with you unless you use a device-level backup or transfer feature.

3. Permissions and reminders

If you enable reminders, the app may use your device's notification permission to show reminders locally. Notification content and timing are controlled through your device settings and the preferences you choose in Hedhi. Hedhi does not need to send your cycle details to a Hedhi server to provide local reminders.

Only grant permissions you are comfortable with. You can review or change app permissions in your device settings.

4. Your device, backups and deletion

Your device's operating system may offer backup, restore or phone-to-phone transfer features. Those are controlled by your device platform and account settings, not by Hedhi's own server. Depending on your settings and the app's backup configuration, app data may be included in a device backup. Check your device backup settings if you want to understand what is backed up.

You can clear app data or uninstall Hedhi using your device's controls. Uninstalling or clearing data may permanently remove local records. Device backups or transferred copies, if any, may need to be managed separately through your device provider.

5. Information when you visit this website

This website is separate from the Hedhi app. It does not ask you to create an account or enter cycle, symptom or other health information. It does not include an online cycle diary or a contact form.

When you visit a website, the hosting provider may process basic technical request information, such as an IP address, browser details, requested page and request time, for delivery, reliability and security. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send.

6. Third-party services

Hedhi does not sell your cycle or wellbeing information. The app's cycle and wellbeing entries are not sent to advertising networks or analytics services by Hedhi.

Your device platform may provide operating-system services such as notifications, backups or app distribution. Those services operate under their own providers' terms and privacy notices. The website is hosted separately; website hosting may process technical request logs as described above.

7. Security

Keeping app records on your device means their protection also depends on your device's security. Use a screen lock, keep your operating system up to date and take care when sharing access to your phone. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your control and choices

You decide what information to enter into Hedhi and which optional features to use. You can manage notifications and app permissions through your device settings. You can remove locally stored app information by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app, bearing in mind that this may permanently delete your records.

For questions about this policy or the website, contact us using the details below.

9. Children

Hedhi is not designed to knowingly collect information from children in circumstances where parental consent or additional safeguards are required. The app's intended age group and store listing should be read together with the applicable platform requirements and local law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or website changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. If a change affects how information is handled, we will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
