1. Overview

Hedhi is a cycle-tracking and wellbeing product. We take privacy seriously because cycle dates, symptoms, mood notes and related information can be personal and sensitive.

Important: This website policy is a public-facing privacy notice template. Before relying on it as the complete Google Play privacy policy, the developer must verify the app's actual data flows, SDKs, backups, analytics, permissions and hosting arrangements and update the app-specific sections below to match the released build.

In this policy, “Hedhi”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the people responsible for the Hedhi website and app.

2. Information on this website

The Hedhi information website is intended to introduce the product and provide contact information. The current website does not provide account registration, a contact form or an online health diary. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and message content.

Our website hosting provider may process basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser type, request time and pages requested, as part of delivering and protecting the website. Confirm the hosting provider's logs, retention period and any analytics or cookie services before publication.

3. Information you enter in the Hedhi app

Depending on the features you use, the app may let you enter information such as:

  • Cycle details, including period start dates, cycle length and period duration.
  • Period logs, flow observations and calendar entries.
  • Symptoms, pain, mood, energy and wellbeing check-ins.
  • Sleep, reminders, tracking preferences and notes you choose to add.
  • Profile preferences, such as a preferred name, language or theme.

Only enter information you are comfortable recording. The exact categories collected by a particular release should be checked against that release before this policy is treated as final.

4. Health-related information and predictions

Cycle and symptom entries may be considered health-related or sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws. We should handle this information with particular care and use it only for the features and purposes explained in the app.

Cycle dates, fertile-window estimates and ovulation estimates are predictions based on the information available and may be inaccurate. Hedhi is a tracking and wellbeing tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or contraception. Do not rely on predictions to make medical or pregnancy-prevention decisions.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. The final app may rely on service providers for functions such as hosting, crash reporting, analytics, notifications or backups. Any provider that receives personal information, the purpose of the transfer and the relevant safeguards must be identified here before release.

We do not currently describe any specific analytics, advertising or cloud-sync provider in this notice because those integrations have not been verified. The developer must add each service actually included in the app and remove this note once the data inventory is complete.

6. Storage and retention

The way app entries are stored, synchronised, backed up and deleted depends on the app's implemented architecture and the services enabled in the installed version. This notice does not assume that data is exclusively stored on your device or that uninstalling the app removes every backup or copy.

Before publishing, document where each category of information is stored, how long it is retained, how users can delete it and whether operating-system backups or connected services may retain copies.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. The specific safeguards in use should be documented by the developer and should not be overstated in this policy.

8. Children

Hedhi is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent or other safeguards are required. The intended age group and any age-screening or parental-consent measures must be aligned with the app's Google Play target-audience settings and applicable law.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To ask a privacy question or make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request before acting. The app should also provide clear in-app instructions for deleting data or accounts if those features are implemented or required.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this page as Hedhi changes. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date above. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we will provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
