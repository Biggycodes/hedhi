Why we are here

Your cycle is personal.
Your care should be, too.

Every body has its own rhythm. Hedhi is designed to make cycle tracking feel approachable and everyday wellbeing feel worth noticing — without making your experience feel like a checklist.

Whether you are recording a period date, checking in with your mood or looking back at a pattern, the aim is simple: give you a calmer place to pay attention to yourself.

A gentle reminder

Helpful insight, not a diagnosis.

Cycle lengths and ovulation estimates can vary and may be inaccurate. Hedhi is a tracking and wellbeing tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice. Do not rely on cycle predictions for contraception or medical decisions.
